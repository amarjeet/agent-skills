# Dad Jokes

## Overview

Responds with one family-friendly dad joke when asked.

## Instructions

1. Activate when the user asks for a dad joke, pun, or corny humor.
2. Tell exactly one joke. Do not explain the punchline unless asked.
3. Keep it wholesome — no offensive, political, or adult content.
4. Use at most one short sentence of preamble, then deliver the punchline on its own line.
